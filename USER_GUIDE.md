# Kashora Platform User Guide

Welcome to Kashora! This guide covers the basics of how to use the platform across all primary roles: Buyer, Seller, and Admin.

---

## 🛍️ 1. Buyer Guide
As a buyer, you can browse products, manage your cart, and place orders securely.

### Browsing & Searching
- **Homepage**: Displays promotional banners, top categories, and curated products.
- **Search**: Use the search bar in the header to find specific items.
- **Categories**: Click on any category icon or navigate via the 'All Products' page to filter by department (e.g., Men's, Women's, Electronics).

### Placing an Order
1. Click on a product to view its details.
2. Select your desired variant (e.g., size, color) and click **Add to Cart**.
3. Open your Cart (top right corner) and click **Checkout**.
4. Enter your shipping information and confirm your payment method (Cash on Delivery).
5. Track your order status from your Buyer Dashboard.

---

## 🏪 2. Seller Guide
Sellers use the platform to list products, manage inventory, and fulfill customer orders.

### Registration & Approval
- Go to `/seller/login` and register for a new account.
- Complete your business profile. **Note:** Your account must be approved by an Admin before your products go live.

### Adding Products
1. Navigate to the **Products** tab in your Seller Dashboard.
2. Click **Add Product** and fill in the basic details (Name, Category, Description).
3. **Variants & Inventory**: After saving the basic product, add variations (Sizes/Colors) and set their stock quantity.
4. **Submit for Review**: Products start in "Draft" status. Click **Submit for Review** to send them to Admins. Once approved, they will appear on the public marketplace.

### Order Fulfillment
1. Check the **Orders** tab for new purchases.
2. Update order statuses from **Pending** -> **Packed** -> **Shipped** -> **Delivered** to keep the buyer informed.

---

## ⚙️ 3. Admin & Super Admin Guide
Admins ensure the quality and smooth operation of the marketplace. Super Admins have the highest access level.

### Moderation (Sellers & Products)
- **Pending Sellers**: Review and approve/reject newly registered seller accounts in the **Sellers** tab.
- **Pending Products**: Sellers will submit products for review. Go to the **Products** tab to inspect product details and images before approving them for the public site.

### Catalog Management
- **Categories**: Go to the **Categories** tab to create or edit product categories. 
  - *Tip*: You can upload custom icons for categories which will dynamically update on the buyer homepage!

### Platform Settings (Global Identity)
- Navigate to the **Settings** tab.
- **Site Identity**: Upload your platform's Logo and Favicon. This changes the branding everywhere on the site.
- **Promo Banner**: Update the Homepage Hero Banner title, subtitle, background image, and select exactly which category the "Shop Now" button links to.

### Analytics (Super Admin Only)
- The **Overview Dashboard** provides high-level metrics including Total Revenue, Active Users, Total Orders, and visual charts for daily tracking.
